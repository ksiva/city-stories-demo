Stories

The queen's inscription

Queen Gotami Balasiri had Cave 3 made at the Nasik Caves. A long inscription from the 19th year of King Pulumavi says she gave it to the Buddhist Sangha. One scholar argues her son dedicated the cave first and she enlarged it.

A shot at the theatre

On 21 December 1909 Anant Kanhere shot A. M. T. Jackson, the Collector of Nashik. It happened at the Vijayanand Theatre during a play. Kanhere was hanged on 19 April 1910.

The march to the temple

On 2 March 1930 B. R. Ambedkar led a protest at the Kalaram Temple. The march was for Dalit entry. The temple had been funded by Sardar Rangarao Odhekar.
